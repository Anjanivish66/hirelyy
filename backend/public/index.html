      Help Center

      Answers to common questions about using Hirely as a job seeker or recruiter.

      Frequently Asked Questions

      How do I create a job seeker account?

      Click Register on the top right, choose "Job Seeker", and fill in your basic details to get started. You can complete your profile afterwards from the dashboard.

      How do I apply for a job or internship?

      Browse jobs or internships from your dashboard, open a listing, and click Apply. You'll need an uploaded resume on your profile to apply.

      How can recruiters post a job?

      Register as a Recruiter, verify your company details, then use "Post a Job or Internship" from the recruiter dashboard.

      I forgot my password. What do I do?

      On the login screen, click "Forgot password?" and follow the steps to reset it using your registered email address.

      How do I track my application status?

      Go to "My Applications" from your job seeker dashboard to see the live status of every job or internship you've applied to.

      Still need help?

      Reach out to our support team any time via the Contact Us page and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

      Privacy Policy

      How we collect, use, and protect your personal information

      About this Notice

      This Privacy Notice explains Hirely's commitment towards managing your personal information and sets out how and when personal data is collected, used, shared, and secured, as well as your choices regarding access and correction of your personal information.

      This Notice applies to data gathered on the platform from registered job seekers and recruiters. For unregistered visitors, it applies to information gathered through publicly available sources and cookies.

      Hirely is committed to protecting the privacy of its users ("You"/"Your"). We strive to provide a safe, secure experience by protecting the personal information that resides with us, and keeping it secure is a top priority.

      What We Collect

      Name, contact details, resume/CV, education and work history, and any other information you choose to add to your profile. For recruiters, we collect company details and job postings. We may also collect usage data such as pages visited and searches made to improve our services.

      How We Use Your Information

      To match you with relevant jobs or candidates, allow recruiters to view applications, send you updates about your applications, and improve the Hirely platform. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

      Your Choices

      You can access, update, or delete your profile information at any time from your account settings. You may also contact our support team to request a copy of your data or ask us to remove your account.
